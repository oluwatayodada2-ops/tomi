About
lensedbytomi

I am a creative mobile videographer with years of experience producing high-quality video content using smartphones and portable gear. Skilled in shooting, editing, color grading, and motion graphics for brands, events, real estate, and social media campaigns. Proficient in CapCut, VN, Premiere Rush, and DaVinci Resolve Mobile, with a strong eye for storytelling, framing, and pacing. Known for delivering fast turnarounds without compromising quality, working efficiently in low-light and on-location conditions. Passionate about helping startups and enterprises build engaging video content that drives views, engagement, and sales. Available for freelance projects, content packages, and on-site shoots anywhere around the world.
